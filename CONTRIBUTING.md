# Contributing critical reviews

The project is in an **open research review** stage and is licensed by material type. The highest-value contributions are reproducible counterexamples, strong prior art, alternate oracles, experimental design improvements and negative findings.

1. Read [REVIEW_GUIDE.md](REVIEW_GUIDE.md).
2. Prefer an issue before a substantive methodology change.
3. Reference concrete clauses and keep one independently testable claim per issue.
4. For suggested edits, preserve the distinction between **known evidence**, **hypothesis**, and **unvalidated suggestion**.
5. Do not publish private, copyrighted, regulated or sensitive material without permission.
6. Maintain the public record: changing a gate or oracle after seeing outcomes requires explicit versioned justification.
